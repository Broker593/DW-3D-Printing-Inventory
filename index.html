Data notes

Sources: Bambu Lab order records (both accounts), Amazon and Ali order histories. Counts are as purchased; DW updates the file as rolls are used.

Spend is supplies only, before tax. Bambu filament figures had the 8% PA + Philadelphia rate backed out; Amazon and Ali are item prices. Awaiting item split, not yet counted: three Bambu orders that mixed printers with the AMS 2 Pro, hotends, a cutting kit, and 21 rolls; the printing-account order; pre-2026 magnets. The P2S and its combo AMS were sold and are excluded.

Bambu swatches are the store’s official colors, sampled per SKU. Third-party swatches are approximations. Support filaments and the Polymaker PET-CF are 0.5 kg rolls; everything else is 1 kg net.

Magnet counts use the manufacturer’s stated per-size split for assorted packs. Not charted: a 2021 FINDMAG 300-pack with no listed size, and magnetic hardware (hooks, clips, sculpture toys).
